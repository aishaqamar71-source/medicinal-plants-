
# Medicinal Plants of Bihar, India

## Project Overview

This project aims to document the diversity of medicinal plants found in Bihar, India, with a focus on their botanical characteristics, traditional uses, distribution, and scientific literature.

The project combines botanical knowledge with literature-based research to develop a structured digital database of medicinal plants.

## Aim

To explore and document medicinal plant diversity in Bihar through botanical identification, scientific literature review, and systematic documentation.

## Objectives

- Document medicinal plants reported from Bihar.
- Record their scientific names and botanical families.
- Study their morphological characteristics.
- Explore their traditional medicinal uses.
- Review scientific publications related to their medicinal properties.
- Develop a structured digital database for educational and research purposes.

## Research Methodology

This project will use secondary research and scientific documentation.

Information will be collected from:
- Scientific research articles.
- Botanical books and floras.
- Published biodiversity records.
- Reliable botanical databases.

Plant information will be organized and cross-checked using scientific references.

## Plant Documentation

Each plant record will include:

1. Common Name
2. Scientific Name
3. Botanical Family
4. Botanical Description
5. Distribution
6. Traditional Uses
7. Relevant Scientific Research
8. References

## Scope of the Project

This project focuses on medicinal plant diversity and botanical documentation in Bihar, India.

It is an educational and literature-based research initiative. It does not involve clinical testing or experimental validation of medicinal properties.
